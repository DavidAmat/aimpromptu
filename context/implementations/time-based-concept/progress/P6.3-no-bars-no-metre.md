# 1. P6.3 — bars, metre and the time signature are removed

Task: P6.3. Decision: D-28.

## 1.1 Why this was needed

A bar is a unit of a beat grid: so many beats, repeated. The package drew barlines over the music as
a reading aid, guessed the metre from where the onsets pile up, and let a reader correct the guess by
hand or jump to bar 34.

None of that has anything to hold on to now. A column is a fixed number of milliseconds and no tempo
takes part in the layout, so there is no period a barline could mark. A guessed metre on a wall-clock
page is a line drawn at a rhythm the page does not have, which is worse than no line, because a
reader trusts a printed barline.

D-28 says the concepts are removed and not hidden, so this deletes them rather than defaulting them
to off.

## 1.2 What was built

Deleted:

- `src/notation/metre.ts`: `detectMetre`, `planBars`, `barForFrame`, `frameForBar`,
  `frameForBarBeat`, `framesToNextBar` and the `Metre` and `BarPlan` types.
- `src/notation/bar-overlay.ts`: `drawBarOverlay`, `BAR_OVERLAY_PADDING`, `BAR_NUMBER_HEIGHT`.
- On the renderer: the `bars` option, `barPlan`, `detectedMetre`, `getBarSettings`, `setBars`,
  `barAt`, `frameForBar` and `framesToNextBar`.
- On the editor: the whole **Bars** tab, its metre picker, its numbering picker, its "downbeat is
  here" and "push to the next bar" actions, the "go to bar" box, `goToBar` and `parseBarBeat`.
- In `notation/spacing.ts`: the sliver a barline reserved on the frame before it.
- In `ruler/frame-ruler.ts`: the bar and beat a revealed timestamp printed beside the clock.
- In `print/render-pages.ts`: the strip reserved above each system for bar numbers.
- `tests/bars.test.ts` in full, and the two metre cases and the bar-number strip case in
  `tests/review-regressions.test.ts`.
- The **Bars** button in the editor artifact.

A vertical line still appears at a key change and at a clef change. That line separates two
spellings and is not a measure; it is drawn by `drawKeyTransition` and `drawClefChange`, which are
untouched.

`docs/api.md` replaces its Bars section with a short statement of what is gone and why, and drops the
three DOM rows for `.grid-barline`, `.grid-bar-number` and the Bars tab's controls.

## 1.3 Decisions taken inside the task

`goToBar` and `parseBarBeat` were deleted rather than kept as a jump by frame. "Go to bar 34" is how
two people name a passage over the phone, but nothing in this app now assigns a number to a bar, so
keeping the box would ask a question that has no answer. The Time tab already jumps to a moment,
which is the wall-clock version of the same thing.

## 1.4 Things I believed that turned out false

I believed the bar overlay was reachable only through the renderer's `bars` option and therefore that
deleting it would touch nothing else. Printing reserved a strip above every system for bar numbers,
and the frame ruler appended "bar 4 beat 2" to a revealed timestamp. Both were dead height and dead
text for a wall-clock score, and both had to be removed by hand.

## 1.5 Tests

The package now runs 42 files and 371 checks, from 43 and 393. `tests/bars.test.ts` held 22 of the 24
removed checks. Every one of them asserted a property of the overlay, so none of them has a meaning
after the deletion: what a 4/4 detector finds in 3/4 music, where a barline falls, that turning bars
on changes no accidental.

`tests/hand-scope.test.ts` asked which toolbox tabs disappear when the reader narrows to one hand. It
named the Bars tab as one of the tabs that belongs to a column rather than to a hand; it now names
the Layout tab, which is another.

`tsc -b`, `eslint`, `prettier` and `npm run build` are clean, and all eight artifacts rebuild.

Looked at in a browser: `deliverables/editor.html` opens with the toolbox tab row missing **Bars**
and reports no console errors.
