# 1. P8.8 — five lines to a page

Task: **P8.8, new.** A correction to [P8.7](P8.7-the-sheet-on-paper.md), asked for on seeing the
first PDF: *"the separation between different lines must be much smaller otherwise we can only
render 3 lines per page, classical music tend to have 5 per page. Please remove the titles on each
page, remove the frames number rendering for the pdf, and put the lines closer to each other. Keep
the same spacing as the actual one for the treble clef - bass clef separation within a given line."*

## 1.1 Why this was needed

P8.7 printed the screen's own vertical rhythm onto A4 and got three systems to a page. Printed music
carries about five. The missing two were not in the music; they were in furniture that only means
something on a screen.

## 1.2 What was built

**`vexflow-v2`, four files.** Three new options on `DrawGrandStaffOptions`, and so on
`RenderPagesOptions` and `renderPages`:

- `frameLabels` (default `true`) — `src/ruler/frame-ruler.ts` skips the `f1300` text when it is off.
  The **guides stay**: they are the wall clock the page is drawn in. The numbers are an address, and
  nobody can click an address on paper.
- `systemPadding` — `src/notation/grand-staff.ts`. The room kept above and below the staves, which
  used to be two module constants. `GRAND_STAFF_TOP_PADDING_BARE = 24` is the new value used when
  there are no frame labels: 52 of the old 60 pixels existed only to hold them.
- `systemGap` — `src/renderer/draw-grand-staff.ts`. Was the hard-coded `SYSTEM_GAP`.

Plus `runningHead` on `renderScorePages`, so the title prints on page one only.

`resolveSystemPadding()` is the single place the padding is worked out, called by
`grandSystemHeight`, by the drawing and by `renderScorePages` before it budgets pages — because a
paginator and a renderer disagreeing about a system's height is how a last line ends up half off the
bottom of a page.

`renderPages()` sets the print defaults: `frameLabels: false`, `runningHead: false`,
`systemPadding: { bottom: 20 }`. **The screen is untouched** — every default is the old value, and
the on-screen sheet renders to exactly the same 1518 × 5462 as before.

**`aitu-frontend/src/components/time/ScorePdfDialog.tsx`** — an **Extra space between lines** slider
(0–48 px, default 0), and the footer now reads "8 pages, up to 5 lines a page, A4 portrait", because
a page count alone does not tell you whether a margin is the thing standing between four lines and
five.

## 1.3 Decisions taken inside the task

**The staff gap is not touched, at all.** Asked for explicitly, and it is the right line to draw:
the distance between a line's two staves is a reading decision the reader already made on the sheet,
while the distance *between* lines is a property of the paper. The two are now separate numbers in
separate options and nothing in the print path can reach the first.

**The dashed guides stay when the numbers go.** They are the only thing on the page that says the
horizontal axis is wall clock, and without them a reader would take the spacing for conventional
note-value spacing and be wrong.

**Slack under the lower staff is a print decision, not a constant.** On screen the page grows
downwards for free, so 40 px under the staff costs nothing; on paper it is the difference between
four systems and five. Twenty is still deeper than the staff itself, and the room a bracket or a
lyric needs is added on top of it either way.

**The slider defaults to zero and is called *extra*.** Every line already keeps room for its ledger
lines and its brackets; this adds air on top of that. Naming it "space between lines" and defaulting
it to 6 would have implied that zero meant staves touching, and 6 px was, exactly, what pushed the
fifth line off the page.

## 1.4 Things believed that turned out false

**That removing the frame numbers would be enough.** It gave back 36 px a line and got four systems
to a page, not five. The last one came down to eleven pixels: at 16 mm ends an A4 page has 983 px of
content, and five systems at the then-current height needed 994. The bottom padding and the default
gap were what closed it.

**That the tighter top padding could be 18 px.** It measured fine on the piece's own first page and
then let a high ledger line poke 0.7 mm above the top margin on page two. Twenty-four holds, and
still leaves five lines a page.

## 1.5 Tests

Re-ran everything from P8.7 §1.5 on the same 4:52 transcription, against the merged tree:

- **Three lines a page became five.** 12 pages → **8**: four on page one (it carries the title),
  five on every full page after it, two on the last.
- **Nothing outside the margins.** Music ink on all eight pages: 14.6 mm from the left, 17.2 mm from
  the right, 16.9 mm from the top, against 14 mm sides and 16 mm ends.
- **No two lines touch.** Scanned every page for horizontal bands of ink: the systems are separated
  everywhere, the tightest white band being 17 px, and the low ledger lines on the last page's first
  system clear the one below it.
- **Every line still carries on where the one above it stopped** — 36 systems tiling f0 → f7304 with
  no gap or overlap across all eight page turns.
- **The screen did not move**: the on-screen sheet renders to the same 1518 × 5462 SVG as before.
- `vitest` in `vexflow-v2`: 382 pass, 43 of 44 files. The one failing file is `deliverables.test.ts`,
  which reads `deliverables/*.html` — not transferred into the test container, and untouched here.
- `npx tsc -b` and `npm run build` clean in both repos; `eslint` clean on the changed files.

## 1.6 Cross-repo change

`vexflow-v2`: `src/ruler/frame-ruler.ts`, `src/notation/grand-staff.ts`,
`src/renderer/draw-grand-staff.ts`, `src/print/render-pages.ts`,
`src/renderer/grid-notation-renderer.ts`, `src/index.ts`, `docs/api.md`. Staying in the frontend was
not possible: every number involved is the drawing package's own geometry. No interface moved —
every new option defaults to the behaviour that was there before.

## 1.7 Left open

- The room reserved under each line for a downward octave bracket (32 px) is reserved on **every**
  line, because systems have to be one height for pages to be a budget of lines. On a score with one
  `8vb` in it, that is 32 px a line paid everywhere.
- A title on page one still costs that page a line. That is how printed scores look, so it is left.
