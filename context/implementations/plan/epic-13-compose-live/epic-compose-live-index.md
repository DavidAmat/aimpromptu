# Epic 13 — Composing live (nice to have)

> **Rewritten 2026-08-12 for the wall-clock model.** See
> [`../wall-clock-rewrite.md`](../wall-clock-rewrite.md).

Build a piece one passage at a time: start from nothing, play a passage, look at its sheet, play it
again until it is right, then put it into the piece. Almost all of it is Epic 11 with the length
restriction removed — an empty piece has nothing after the insertion point to protect.

Read first: [`../wall-clock-rewrite.md`](../wall-clock-rewrite.md) and Task 11.1.1, which owns the
session and the splice.

## Story 13.1 — Live composition workflow

- Task 13.1.1 compose live: an empty piece, a passage stage, and the three ways to place a passage.

## Exit criteria

Manual trial: build a short two-passage piece from nothing, one of the passages played at half speed
and scaled to the length it should occupy, and read the result as one sheet.
