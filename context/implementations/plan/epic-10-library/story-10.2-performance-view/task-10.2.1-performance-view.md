# Task 10.2.1 — Performance view

`/library/play/:id`: what a pianist has open on stage.

## Subtask 10.2.1.1 — Read-only render

A blank page with only the rendered score (Task 9.1.1 document via the standard renderer). No editing affordances. Fast load (pre-generated score document).

## Subtask 10.2.1.2 — Overlay toggles

Small unobtrusive toggles to hide/show metadata overlays: lyrics, finger numbers (once Epic 12 exists; toggles ship disabled until then).

## Subtask 10.2.1.3 — Navigation

Back-to-library button; when opened from a playlist, a Next button (Task 10.3.1). Interaction model: scroll down, adjust browser zoom, done.

## Acceptance

Manual trial: open a promoted piece, toggle overlays, scroll through it full-screen.
