# P7.1 and P6.1 — the Rhythm screen, and the renderer taking its figures from the payload

Tasks: P7.1 `[AITU]`, P6.1 `[GN]` in part. Decisions implemented: D-09, D-10, D-11, D-20, D-22.
Date: 2026-08-06. Status: the walk works in the app.

## 1. What a person can now do

Load an audio, transcribe it, open **Rhythm**, click the gap they recognise, say what it is, and read
the staff that comes out. That is the whole model in one screen, in the order it makes sense.

The screen is deliberately one page rather than three. Naming a gap and seeing the sheet are the same
decision seen twice, and splitting them across tabs would make trying a second name feel expensive
when it costs nothing.

## 3. Decisions taken inside the task

**The renderer gained one option rather than a rewrite.** `printedFigureFor(hand, onsetFrame)` beats
every rule in the package, and returning nothing falls back to what it did before. That is the whole
of P6.1's intent, and it means the 1.x screens keep working untouched. Deleting the candidate search
belongs to the task that removes those screens.

**The bars are a fixed width.** The first version drew each pile as wide as its basin, which made a
common gap with loose timing look like a rare wide one. How often a gap happens is the height; how
loosely it was played is a different question and does not belong on the same axis.

**The biggest pile starts selected.** It is where a reader looks first. Nothing is committed by it:
the sheet only appears after a name is given.

**The sheet stops where the music stops.** A recording often runs on for several seconds after the
playing ends, and drawing that gave a second system holding nothing but rests. `trim_to_music` cuts
the score at the last sounding column. The recording is untouched and playback still uses its full
length; this decides only where the page ends.

## 4. Things I believed that turned out false

I believed the frontend could be checked by reading it. It cannot. The Upload / Input tab still
carried a BPM field, a note-figure dropdown and a tempo calculator, all meaningless under this model,
and I only found that when the supervisor opened the page. Every screen in this refactor is now run
and looked at before it is written up: the sources are copied into a container, both services are
started there, and Chromium is driven with Playwright.

I also expected the renderer to need the whole of Phase 5 before it could draw a wall-clock score. It
does not. Constant column widths draw a correct page; silence compression and the merged timeline
improve it but are not what stood between the payload and a staff.

## 5. Tests

package's suite is otherwise unchanged.

`aitu-backend`: `test_the_sheet_stops_where_the_music_stops` covers the trim, and the existing suite
was updated where it asserted the untrimmed length.

Checked on screen: the plot, the naming, the staff, and renaming the same pile as a different figure.
