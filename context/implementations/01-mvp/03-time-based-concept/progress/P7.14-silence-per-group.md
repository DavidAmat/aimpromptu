# 1. P7.14 — a stretch where nothing happens stops taking a page to say so

Task: **P7.14, a fix.** Decision: D-23.

## 1.1 Why this was needed

David sent a picture of the end of the piece: about fifteen long notes spread from one side of the
line to the other, with the dashed lines a long way apart and almost nothing between them. A reader
looking at that page sees a lot of space and reads it as a lot of music.

D-23 says a run of columns nothing is struck in collapses to **a small padding per frame group**.
The package was charging it per *column*: `EMPTY_FRAME_SPACES`, 2.4 px, for every empty column.
At 40 ms a redonda covers about thirty-four columns, so thirty-three slivers stood between one
notehead and the next, which is roughly eighty pixels of nothing. Multiplied by fifteen notes, that
is the whole line.

`silenceGroupPx` has been in the contract and in every payload since Phase 3, and nothing read it.

## 1.2 What was built

`measureFrameWidths` takes `frameGroup` and `silenceGroupPx`. After every column has asked for what
it draws, the columns of a group that are still empty share one budget between them instead of each
keeping its own sliver. A column carrying a notehead, an accidental, a tied continuation or a
boundary block is untouched, so nothing can collide.

Measured on the real recording, at the default 25 columns to a group and 6 px of silence per group:

| Stretch | Onsets | Columns | Page width |
|---|---|---|---|
| The sparse tail, after f7300 | 25 | 361 | 55 px |
| A dense stretch, f900 to f1000 | 61 | 84 | 241 px |

The closest two consecutive onsets in that tail are 22 px apart, so the noteheads have their room.
Before the change the same 361 columns cost about 870 px, which is most of a line.

The whole piece is now eight systems where it was ten, and the difference is all in the sparse
passages: a dense group has few empty columns and they were already narrow.

**The browser's own text selection is switched off over the score.** Shift-clicking a second group
of columns made the browser select everything between the two clicks and paint it in the highlight
colour, which looks like an error and hides the music until the next click clears it. A score is a
drawing, so the SVG is marked `user-select: none`, as is the box the sheet sits in.

## 1.3 Things I believed that turned out false

I believed the widths were wrong because something in Phases 5 and 6 had changed them. Nothing had.
The per-column sliver has been there since P5.2 and was wrong from the day it was written: P5.2
implemented D-23's compression at the wrong level and nothing caught it, because the piece it was
checked against has no long sparse passage near the beginning.

## 1.4 Tests

`vexflow-v2/tests/time-to-x.test.ts` gained two checks: a group holding one long chord comes out less
than half the width it does without the budget, and a group full of notes is unaffected because its
columns are not empty.

Package: 44 files, 377 checks, none failing. `tsc -b`, `eslint`, `prettier` and both builds clean.
Version **0.30.0**. Frontend: `tsc -b` and `eslint` clean.

Looked at in a browser against the real recording: the tail that filled a whole line now takes about
a third of one, the notes in it are evenly spaced and none overlap, and shift-clicking two groups
leaves no orange text selection behind.

## 1.5 Left open

`EMPTY_FRAME_SPACES` is still the per-column sliver for a host that supplies no layout hints, which
is what the editor artifact uses. That is correct for it: an editor addresses single columns.
