The new sheet: figures, grace notes and alteraciones

Implementation 03, Phase 2. Passages of My library written by the algorithm of the new sheet: no compases, no tempo, no puntillos, no silencios. The notes are drawn by Verovio only to check the names, figures and grace notes: the engine and the spacing of the new sheet come in Phase 3. Hands come from the saved hands and the hand split; the LLM decides them in Phase 4.
